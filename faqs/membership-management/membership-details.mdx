---
title: "How can I change my membership details?"
description: "Steps to upgrade or downgrade a customer's membership in Kenko"
---

To change a customer's membership:

- Go to `Contacts` \> `Customers`.
- Open the desired customer profile.
- Navigate to `Subscriptions`.
- Click the `⋮` (three-dot) menu.
- Select **Change Membership**.
- Choose either to upgrade or downgrade the membership.
